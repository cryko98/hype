The Lore

How Pepe turned mint

A story in four chapters, about leverage, rock bottom and the deepest pool in the pond.

Chapter I

Rekt at 3 AM

Pepe was an ordinary green frog with an ordinary green-frog problem: 100x leverage and zero risk management. Every long got liquidated. Every short got squeezed. Instant noodles became his whole diet.

The chart was red. His eyes were red. Even his wallet was red, which shouldn't even be possible.

Chapter II

The Hyper Liquid Pool

One sleepless night he followed a strange mint glow to the edge of the pond and found a pool of pure, glowing hyper liquid. Its order book was so deep that nothing in it could ever sink.

He leaned in for a closer look, slipped, and fell in backwards. He was finally fully in.

Chapter III

Mint Condition

He climbed out mint. Calm eyes, steady hands, thumb permanently up. Every drop of that liquid had soaked into his skin.

Hyper Pepe can't be liquidated anymore, because he is the liquidity. Red candles are just weather now, and funding rates are just a feeling.

Chapter IV

Pill-Powered to Solana

A frog this liquid can't stay in one pond. Hyper Pepe grabbed the nearest green pill, lit the fuse and launched straight onto Solana through pump.fun.

It was a fair launch: no presale, no VCs, no secret team bags. It's just $HYPE and the frogs who show up. The chart only goes one way in his dreams, and he dreams in mint.
